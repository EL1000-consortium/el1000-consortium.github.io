Who We Are

EL1000 is an international, community-governed research consortium bringing together researchers who study children's everyday language environments across diverse cultural and linguistic communities.

By coordinating research across multiple corpora, EL1000 supports large-scale comparative studies of children's early language experiences.

Our Mission

Our mission is to advance understanding of the diversity of children's language environments through collaborative, cross-cultural research.

We aim to support transparent, reproducible, and scientifically rigorous analyses while respecting the contributions and responsibilities of participating research teams.

Our Research Approach

EL1000 brings together standardized automated measures derived from daylong audio recordings collected in different communities.

These measures can include estimates of adult speech, child vocalizations, and conversational interactions, depending on the available processing methods and corpus.

EL1000 does not distribute raw audio recordings. Research use of derived measures is subject to consortium procedures and approvals.

Collaborative Governance

EL1000 operates through community-based governance. Research proposals are reviewed by a board, and participating data curators are informed about approved projects.

This approach is designed to support meaningful collaboration, responsible data use, and recognition of substantive scientific contributions.
