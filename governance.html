Our Governance Principles

EL1000 is an international, community-governed research consortium. Its governance framework is designed to support scientific collaboration, responsible use of derived data, and meaningful participation by contributing research teams.

The consortium seeks to balance the benefits of large-scale comparative research with respect for the communities and researchers represented in participating corpora.

The Community Board

The EL1000 board oversees research proposal review and helps coordinate approved projects.

The board works to ensure that proposed research aligns with the consortium's scientific goals and established procedures.

Information about board membership and administrative responsibilities will be published here as it is confirmed.

Research Proposal Process

Researchers interested in using EL1000 derived measures submit a research proposal for review.

1

Proposal Submission

Researchers describe their research questions, planned analyses, and requested measures.

2

Board Review

The community board reviews the proposal under consortium procedures.

3

Curator Notification

Following approval, participating corpus curators are notified and given an opportunity to opt out of the specific project or express interest in contributing to it.

4

Project Coordination

Approved projects proceed in accordance with consortium procedures, including applicable curator decisions and data-use requirements.

Role of Data Curators

Data curators contribute scientific and contextual expertise concerning their respective corpora.

After a research proposal is approved, curators are informed about the project and may opt out of that particular use of their corpus under the consortium's established procedures.

Curators may also express interest in substantive scientific collaboration on approved projects.

Authorship and Recognition

EL1000 aims to recognize meaningful intellectual contributions to research.

Contributing a corpus does not automatically confer authorship on every publication. Authorship is based on substantive contributions to the relevant research project.

Appropriate acknowledgement and citation of contributing datasets and research teams remain important.

Responsible Data Use

EL1000 does not distribute raw audio recordings through the consortium. Research projects use approved derived measures and associated metadata, subject to applicable procedures.

The consortium promotes responsible research practices and respect for participating communities.

Governance Inquiries

For questions about research proposals, consortium governance, or curator participation, contact the EL1000 board.
