Propose a research project

Researchers interested in using EL1000 derived measures can contact the consortium to discuss a research proposal.

Preparing your proposal

A research proposal should outline:

  • The research questions and scientific motivation.
  • The planned methods and analyses.
  • The derived measures and metadata required for the project.
  • The research team and anticipated scientific contributions.
  • The intended outputs, including publications or presentations.

Proposal review

Proposals are reviewed by the EL1000 community board under consortium governance procedures.

Following approval, relevant corpus curators are notified in accordance with consortium procedures.

Please contact the EL1000 board for current proposal submission requirements.
