What Do the Corpora Contain?

EL1000 works with automated measures derived from daylong audio recordings collected during children's everyday activities.

Adult Speech

Automated estimates of speech in children's everyday environments.

Child Vocalizations

Automated measures of children's vocal activity.

Conversational Interactions

Interaction-related measures where appropriate processing outputs are available.

The available measures vary by corpus and processing method.

Corpus Directory
