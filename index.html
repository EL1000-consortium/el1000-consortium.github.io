About EL1000

EL1000 brings together researchers studying children's everyday language environments across diverse communities worldwide.

Our goal is to enable large-scale, cross-cultural research through standardized automated measures.

Our Research Approach

EL1000 uses derived measures from daylong audio recordings, including automated estimates of speech and child vocalizations.

Raw audio recordings are not shared through the consortium. Research access to derived measures is governed by EL1000 procedures.

How to Participate

Researchers can contribute eligible derived measures or submit proposals for projects using consortium data, subject to EL1000 governance and approval procedures.

Get in touch

Community Governance

A community-based board reviews research proposals and oversees approved projects. Data curators can opt out of individual projects under the consortium's established procedures.
